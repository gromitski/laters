# Terms and acceptable use

## Status

Implemented, published and accepted as the bounded `v0.5.3` slice. This is a public policy and
documentation slice; it
does not change storage, sync, Google OAuth status or infrastructure.

## Product contract

- The hosted app has plain-English Terms at `/terms/`, linked from the application menu, privacy
  policy and README.
- The Terms distinguish the free hosted app from source code released under the MIT Licence.
- Both public policies state plainly and prominently that the Laters maintainer cannot see a user's
  reading list; optional sync stores it only in that user's private Google Drive application data.
- Users are responsible for their devices, browser, Google Account, saved links, third-party terms,
  reasonable backups and ordinary connectivity or account costs.
- Acceptable use prohibits unlawful or harmful use, malicious links or code, security interference,
  unreasonable automation, limit evasion, impersonation and use as a commercial, shared or high-risk
  data service.
- Data-risk wording addresses loss and unauthorised disclosure caused by user-controlled systems,
  third parties, misuse or circumstances outside the maintainer's reasonable control.
- Liability wording preserves non-excludable responsibilities and remedies, including negligence
  causing death or personal injury, fraud and applicable reasonable-care-and-skill duties. It does
  not use an absolute disclaimer.
- Withdrawal or restriction is tied to stated security, abuse, legal, platform, maintenance or
  project-ending reasons, with reasonable notice where practical.

## Basis and limits

The Consumer Rights Act 2015 requires consumer terms and notices to be fair and transparent and bars
excluding liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence. Current Competition and
Markets Authority guidance also warns against broad disclaimers and unexplained legal shorthand,
and advises making significant terms clear and prominent:

- [Consumer Rights Act 2015, Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/part/2)
- [CMA: Writing a fair contract for customers](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/writing-a-fair-contract-for-customers)

The wording is therefore deliberately specific about which risks belong to users or third parties
and which legal protections remain. It is proportionate policy drafting for a free personal project,
not a claim of legal immunity or a substitute for independent legal advice if the service later
becomes commercial, collects account data or changes operator.

## Exclusions

- No click-through acceptance, account registration, payment or subscription.
- No change to the privacy data flow beyond cross-linking the policies.
- No production Google Cloud project, public OAuth approval, quotas or billing configuration.
- No user suspension system or Laters-operated backend.

## Acceptance

- The Terms page is legible at mobile and desktop widths, keyboard accessible and covered by the
  same restrictive static-page security policy as Privacy.
- Public-build auditing requires both policy pages, their cross-links, the public contact and the
  central responsibility boundaries.
- The complete test, type, build, privacy and public-build gates pass.
- After publication, the maintainer reviews the Terms in the app drawer and confirms that the tone,
  responsibility split and at-your-own-risk explanation match the intended public product.
