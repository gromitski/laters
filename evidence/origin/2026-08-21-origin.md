# Original project idea

Archived on 2026-08-21 from the maintainer's rough intent. No redactions were required.

> I want to build an extremely simple personal read-later PWA.
>
> I regularly find articles on my Android phone that I want to read later, but I do not want a complex bookmarking or knowledge-management system.
>
> The intended interaction is:
>
> 1. Find an article on Android.
> 2. Use Android's Share menu.
> 3. Share the article to the installed PWA.
> 4. Save the article locally.
> 5. Show saved articles newest first when I open the app.
> 6. Display a compact item with its title, relative saved time and a delete action.
> 7. Link the title directly to the original article.
> 8. Delete the item when requested.
>
> This is a personal MVP, not a public product.
>
> The preferred technical direction is a PWA using Android Web Share Target and IndexedDB, requesting persistent browser storage where supported. There should be no backend, authentication, accounts, cloud database or sync initially. There should also be no tags, folders, archive workflow or AI features.
>
> Keep the architecture expandable through a small storage abstraction.
